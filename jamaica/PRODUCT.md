# JAMAICA

Inferred from the owner's written brief (no interview held); assumptions are marked.

## What it is
A premium fashion e-commerce store selling clothing, accessories (watches, sunglasses, bags, jewelry) and footwear in Brazil, with real 3D/360° product views, an outfit builder and virtual try-on. Prices in BRL; payment by PIX, credit and debit card.

## Who uses it
- **Shoppers** in Brazil, mostly on phones (assumption), browsing, comparing and buying.
- **Store team** (owner, managers, staff) running the catalogue, stock, orders, customers and promotions from the admin dashboard, mostly on a laptop during the working day (assumption), sometimes on a phone to check an order.

## Surfaces and modes
- Storefront (homepage, departments, product pages): Persuade.
- Checkout, cart, account: Operate.
- Admin dashboard: Operate. Scanability and speed of routine tasks (add a product, update stock, mark an order shipped) beat expression; brand shows in type and colour only.
- Help and policy pages: Read.

## Brand
Black, jade/emerald, white, subtle gold. One typeface (Archivo variable; the width axis carries display vs. text). Square corners. Premium, modern, cinematic on the storefront; calm and exact in the admin.

## Non-negotiables (from the brief)
- No fake functionality: no simulated payments, no fake AI, no pretend 360° from a flat photo.
- Administrators manage everything without code, and changes appear in the store immediately.
- Customer photos for try-on only with explicit consent, deletable at any time.
